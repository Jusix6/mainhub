## What it is

A little frog sits on a lily pad in the middle of a swamp, staring at you. All
around it, flies are buzzing. But these aren't normal flies: every single one
is a keyboard key with wings. Press the right key and the frog snaps it out of
the air with its tongue. Press the wrong one and you lose a heart.

FrogType is a cozy but surprisingly challenging typing game with hand-drawn
art, six boss fights and a frog that really, really wants to wear a crown.

I built the whole thing in seven days for a video on my channel. The video is
linked above, the game is free on itch.io, in the browser or as a Windows
download.

## What awaits you

- **30 levels in 6 worlds.** Single keys, key combos you have to press at the
  same time, flies with a ticking timer, words, UPPER and lower case, and numbers.
- **6 boss fights, each with its own twist.** Morph Fly keeps changing into new
  keys. Color Boss: only catch the flies in its color, while it keeps switching.
  Word Boss: longer and longer words, less and less time. Caps Fly: mIxEd CaSe
  words, hold on to that Shift key. Pi Boss: type the first 100 digits of Pi,
  protected by guardian flies. Boss Fly: the grand finale that knows every trick.
- **Dress up your frog.** Every letter you type goes into your wallet. Spend it
  at the fishing dock shop on hats, glasses, a moustache and bow ties.
- **Checkpoints after every boss**, so you can always continue from the last
  world you reached.
- **Hand-drawn art and original music & sound.**
- **A secret hidden in the main menu.** Can you find it?

## Controls

- Type the letters, numbers and combos on the flies
- Esc pauses, F11 toggles fullscreen
- Click the sign on the left for the menu, the dock on the right for the shop
