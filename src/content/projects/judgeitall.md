## What it was

For a short while in summer 2025, JudgeItAll was a German blog where I rated
random things with a straight face: Coca-Cola glasses, water, whatever came to
mind. Each post also went up as a video on the JudgeItAll YouTube channel, so
the two videos there are the blog posts in video form.

## Why it stopped

I gave up on it pretty quickly. The WordPress site is gone, the videos are
still up.

## Will it come back?

Probably not. But it is on ice rather than deleted, so never say never.
