## What it is

Forgot? helps you stop forgetting the things that matter, from watering plants
to paying bills. Create routines and get reminded exactly when it counts.

## How you schedule

- Fixed times, several times a day, or flexible
- Every few days or weeks
- Specific weekdays, like every Sunday
- Specific days of the month, like the 1st

## Stay on top

- "Up next" overview and daily progress
- Completion rate and history
- Quick check-off, even from the notification
- Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets

## Made simple

- Local reminders, no account needed
- iCloud sync across your devices
- English and German, light and dark mode

## Premium

Up to five routines are free. Forgot Premium unlocks unlimited routines and
widgets, as a yearly subscription or a one-time purchase.
