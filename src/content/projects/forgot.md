## What it is

Forgot? is an iOS app for building routines you keep. It reminds you of the
small things that make a day work, without turning your life into a checklist.

## Why I built it

Most habit apps punish you for missing a day. Forgot? is built around the
opposite idea: getting back on track should feel easy, not shameful.
