A daily game: **[Paint the Flag](https://fun.jxsi.ch/flag)**. You get the
name of a country and an empty outline, and you paint its flag from memory.
Then you see how close you got.

## How it works

- Four flags a day, one per level: **Easy, Medium, Hard and Impossible**.
  Everybody gets the same four, and they change at midnight Swiss time.
- One scored try per level and day, a streak, and a countdown to tomorrow.
- **Practice** mode as often as you like.
- Your score is counted per colour area, so a slightly wobbly stripe does not
  ruin a good flag. The share card shows yours next to the real one.
- Short link: **jxsi.ch/flag**.

## What was hard

The flags themselves. Easy, Medium and Hard are 66 flags I built by hand
out of simple shapes, so an exact copy really is 100 %. *Impossible* is
every one of the 197 countries, imported from public-domain SVG files by a
script. That is where all the coats of arms, eagles and tiny details live.

## Changed after launch

At first I sorted the levels by how much there is to paint. It felt wrong
right away: a simple flag nobody knows is not *easy*. So the levels are now
sorted by how well known a flag is first, and by the painting work second.
Japan, Switzerland and France are Easy. Mali, Gabon and Qatar are Hard, even
though they are only a few stripes.
